Reviewed 7–8 September 2026. Findings only. Application source and existing project documentation were not edited. This report, temporary diagnostic scripts and fresh screenshots were added.

**The largest graphical gain is fixing the overlapping inner-envelope surfaces.** Correct the remaining labels and cutaway geometry next, then give cell-view focus a stable depth allowance and improve the handover to small bodies. Keep the procedural surface system. The overview reads clearly as an anatomical reconstruction, but the striped boundary, repeated extruded sheets and deep-zoom blur prevent it from consistently reading as a recorded specimen.

The writing's main problem is evidence promoted beyond its source: model choices acquire a “measured” badge, historical general descriptions become species measurements, and “not measured” claims survive newer papers already in the repository.

Costs below are estimates for someone familiar with this code. High confidence means a direct contradiction, reproducible calculation or controlled render; medium means a visual judgment or interpretation requiring more evidence.

### 6. Deep-zoom DOF erases newly resolved detail; give it a floor

**Where:** [optics.jsx:67](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/optics.jsx:67), [App.jsx:81](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/App.jsx:81). **What:** Circle of confusion is (dist-focus)/dist × aperture. At 900 px height, maximum blur radius is 11.7 px; one-pixel blur is reached at roughly 5% relative depth error. **Why:** Rules 3 and 5. Tomogram display magnification need not change the depth being inspected. The objective metaphor obstructs the ultrastructure view.

**Do:** Add a cell-only world-depth allowance before the blur roll-off. Evaluate an explicitly display-chosen half-band of roughly 300–500 nm, or selected-body depth extent plus a margin. Preserve broader whole-cell depth cueing. Do not call the chosen floor a measured slice thickness. **Cost:** half a day including crop comparisons.

**Confidence:** high for mechanism and blur; medium for the proposed initial tolerance. This is my answer to the open DOF question.

### 8. The shader has two remaining gaps in its band-limit contract

**Where:** [optics.jsx:419](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/optics.jsx:419), [640](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/optics.jsx:640), [CellSection.jsx:358](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/CellSection.jsx:358), [494](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/src/CellSection.jsx:494).

**What:** When only the base fbm octave survives, sum/norm cancels its fade weight: w×n/w = n. Contrast persists until w ≤ 0.002, where the loop breaks and returns neutral. The last surviving octave therefore does not fade smoothly to neutral.

Also, finest limits fbm but not the independent grain. Antenna grain period is 1/0.35 = **2.86 nm** despite finest = 6. Thylakoid grainScale = 0.11 with fiber.y = 2.4 gives a shortest-axis nominal period of **3.79 nm**, below its 5 nm floor. Warping additionally makes nominal periods approximate.

**Why:** Rule 5. These are implementation exceptions to the chosen two-limit policy.

**Do:** Preserve per-octave fading and separate persistences. Give the final surviving band terminal coverage that its normalization cannot cancel. Apply the specimen limit to grain too, including anisotropy. **Cost:** half to one day including animated grazing-angle crops.

**Confidence:** high algebraically. Static crops do not prove temporal crawling, and I do not claim they do. The mathematical counterexample is new evidence for tightening the existing policy; it does not justify restoring the rejected base-octave fade of the whole sum.

## Optics, lighting, palette and performance

**Optics.** Fog does substantial whole-cell depth work. The 18,450 nm home distance lies well inside the 12,800–25,500 nm fog interval. At deep zoom the inspected foreground is before fog onset, so DOF is the more immediate loss of detail.

AO is a six-neighbour screen-depth comparison, not a normal-aware estimate of enclosure. Its radius is about 4.7 px at 900 px height. Three fully contributing samples can multiply colour by about 0.525 before the sharpness gate. It can darken a sloping face as well as a crevice, and disappears as blur reduces sharpness. Rendered seams provide useful separation, but broad faces remain similarly valued. I would not raise 0.95. Recheck AO after adding a DOF floor, because more pixels will regain AO too. No AO-disabled comparison was run, so I do not attribute all muted mid-tones to AO.

Glare, aberration, vignette and grain are secondary at home composition. Additive glare matches its stated intent. I found no reason for wholesale retuning.

**Lighting.** The rig has three directional lights plus a hemisphere light. In [baseline](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/docs/shots/review-light-baseline.png) versus [warm fill disabled](C:/Users/Gianluca/OneDrive/Desktop/Algae 3D interactive/docs/shots/review-light-no-fill.png), the whole-cell change is small. The fill is not undoing the key enough to be the first fix.

The claim in sites 57 that a 15 nm riser is read by its shadow is unsupported: wall and lamella meshes do not cast or receive those shadows. The key's 22,400 nm shadow span across 2048 texels is about 10.9 nm/texel anyway. Correct the explanation to lighting/normal contrast and screen-space depth cues; do not add expensive shadows merely to vindicate prose. Current shadows chiefly serve the floor and the few casting bodies.

**Palette.** Keep the broad hue families: red cyanophycin, pale gas vesicles, faceted ochre carboxysomes and blue-violet polyphosphate are separable. The core and envelope are recognizable. Lipid bodies remain subdued olive-brown balls; roughness 0.86 gives little liquid sheen, but correcting their claimed identity comes first. DNA and ribosomes share a warm hue and merge into speckles at near LOD; address the handover before inventing more colours. The wall-selection render distinctly shows the five treads.

**Cost.** Integrated graphics are a plausible concern, not a measured failure. SwiftShader shot times cannot predict hardware FPS.

Each noise call hashes eight corners. General organic surfaces can execute three warp noises, seven fbm noises and two grain noises: up to **12**, not universally 11. Current thylakoid settings permit about five main fbm octaves at the specimen cutoff; the carpet adds another fbm and noise sample, for roughly 14 calls before standard physical lighting. Pixel limits reduce actual counts.

The cheapest candidate saving is to skip both grain samples when their contribution has vanished, then skip carpet work when uCarpet is zero. Preserve resolved octaves and separate persistences. A compiler may already eliminate some work; profile before assigning a speedup. **Cost:** a few hours plus GPU profiling. The septum also tests all 56 pore centres per fragment, and the compositor performs 29 texture reads with AO enabled. Profile these together. If a quality tier is needed, cap DPR before removing anatomy. No measured FPS or percentage speedup is claimed.
